# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard — Excel
An interactive **Excel Dashboard** designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance and convert transactional data into meaningful business insights.
The project uses **Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, Slicers, and Conditional Formatting** to analyze sales trends, product performance, customer purchasing patterns, and location-wise performance.


## Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales data and create an interactive dashboard that helps users quickly understand key business metrics and sales patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into:
* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Bill
* Best-Selling Coffee Products
* Monthly Sales Trends
* Category-Wise Sales
* Location-Wise Sales Performance
* Customer Purchase Trends
* Peak Sales Periods
The dashboard is interactive, allowing users to explore different aspects of the business using slicers and visualizations.


## Business Objective
The main goal of this project is to transform raw coffee shop transaction data into an easy-to-understand analytical dashboard.

The analysis helps answer questions such as:
* What is the overall sales performance?
* Which products are selling the most?
* Which categories generate the highest sales?
* Which months perform better?
* Which locations generate higher sales?
* When are the peak sales periods?
* What purchasing patterns can be identified from the transaction data?

## Dashboard KPIs
The dashboard includes important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

### Total Sales
Measures the overall revenue generated from coffee shop transactions.

### Total Orders
Shows the total number of customer orders/transactions.

### Average Bill
Represents the average amount spent per order.

### Best-Selling Products
Identifies products with higher sales performance.
These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.


## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis
Monthly sales are analyzed to identify:
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Changes in sales over time
* Seasonal sales patterns

A monthly sales visualization makes it easier to understand how revenue changes throughout the period.


### 2. Product Performance Analysis
The dashboard analyzes product-level sales performance to identify:
* Best-selling products
* Low-performing products
* Product demand patterns
* Products contributing significantly to overall sales

This analysis can help businesses understand customer preferences and make better inventory decisions.


### 3. Category-Wise Sales Analysis
Sales are analyzed across different product categories.
This helps identify:
* High-performing categories
* Categories contributing more to sales
* Category-level purchasing patterns

Category analysis can support product planning and promotional decisions.


### 4. Location-Wise Performance
The dashboard provides location-level sales analysis to compare business performance across different locations.
This can help identify:
* High-performing locations
* Low-performing locations
* Differences in sales performance
* Areas requiring further investigation


### 5. Customer Purchase Trends
Transaction data is analyzed to understand customer purchasing behavior.
The analysis focuses on:
* Order patterns
* Product preferences
* Purchase frequency
* Average spending patterns

These insights can help understand customer demand and purchasing behavior.


### 6. Peak Sales Analysis
Sales patterns are analyzed across time periods to identify periods with higher customer activity.
This can be useful for:
* Staff scheduling
* Inventory planning
* Promotional campaigns
* Operational planning


## Interactive Dashboard
The dashboard uses **Excel Slicers** to make the analysis interactive.
Users can filter the dashboard and dynamically explore different parts of the sales data.
This makes the dashboard more useful than a static report because users can investigate specific segments of the business.
